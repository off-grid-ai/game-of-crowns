# Where the demand is (researched 2026-11-05)

## The pools (SteamSpy owner ranges and Steam reviews; Workshop API)

| Who | Size | What they're missing |
|---|---|---|
| **"Watch a world" players**: WorldBox, Ages of Conflict, Fantasy Map Simulator | WorldBox 1–2M owners, 44k reviews, 95% positive. Ages of Conflict 200–500k. Fantasy Map Sim 100–200k. | The same complaint in all three: **boring after a few hours, passive, no goals, shallow diplomacy, little agency.** |
| **Game of Thrones politics fans** | The CK3 "A Game of Thrones" mod: **667k current subscribers, 877k lifetime, 52k favourites.** That's people installing a 13 GB mod for a hard game just to get Westeros politics. | No standalone game does it. CK3 is the only route, with a steep learning curve. |
| **Medieval look**: Manor Lords, Medieval Dynasty | Manor Lords 2–5M owners. Medieval Dynasty 1–2M. | They prove the look sells; neither is about ruling a whole realm of houses. |
| **Talking, remembering characters** | Mantella (an AI-dialogue mod for Skyrim) has a loyal following. | Mainstream players dislike generative AI: games that disclose it get lower recommendation rates. A niche, not the lead. |

Review summaries (vaporlens.app, which groups Steam reviews by theme):
- **WorldBox:** "gets boring after a few hours", "shallow/empty late game"; players want "deeper civilization systems and meaningful diplomacy" and "clear goals and direction".
- **Fantasy Map Simulator:** "passive, boring gameplay"; wants "more player agency over the simulation".
- **Ages of Conflict:** "gets boring after a few hours", "shallow simulation", "AI behaves irrationally"; wants "more control over wars and peace treaties".

## What this means
**The demand is WorldBox players who love watching a world and get bored when nothing is at stake.** That's over a million people saying, in reviews, what they want: goals, diplomacy that matters, a say in the world. Our game is exactly that: the world still runs itself, but you're the King, every choice costs something, and the houses remember.

Game of Thrones is the flavour that pulls a second crowd (877k installed a mod for it). Westworld minds are the depth that keeps people, but they're not what to lead with.

**The pitch, in the players' own words:**
> WorldBox, but you're the King: the world still runs itself, and now your choices matter.
