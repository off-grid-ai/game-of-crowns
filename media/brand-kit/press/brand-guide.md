# Brand guide

## Name

Write the full name as **Game of Crowns** in prose and **GAME OF CROWNS** in the title artwork. Write the short name as **GoC**: capital G, lower-case o, capital C.

Say GoC as “gee-oh-see”.

## Design direction

Serious, restrained, historical, and intriguing. Give the type room. Use small crown details and fine lines. Keep metal texture quiet.

The selected full title is option C. Its logo is custom raster artwork, not an editable font. Keep the original letter shapes when you place it in a layout.

Use the GoC mark for small square spaces. Use the full title for key art and a first introduction to the game.

## Colour palette

These values are working layout colours chosen to support the artwork. The painted metal and sky have natural variation.

| Colour | Hex | Use |

| Charcoal | `#151719` | Main dark field. |

## Logo use

- Keep the aspect ratio.
- Leave space around the mark at least equal to the height of the small crown.
- Use the transparent PNG over a calm area.
- Keep it separate from a face or a busy building.
- Check the full title at the final display size.
- Use GoC when the full name becomes too small to read.
- Keep the crown small.
- Keep letters free of added gems, flames, gloss, and heavy bevels.

## Image use

Use the landscape art for the wider realm and the portrait art for narrow placements. Keep the castle, skyline, and title inside the intended crop.

Use the X file for a wide profile header. The YouTube banner has a central text area. Check the platform's upload preview because profile overlays and crops can change.

The Discord banner has no title text, so the server's own title can remain clear.

## Small icons

Use the PNG, ICO, or ICNS export that matches the job. Keep an unmasked square source. Platforms apply their own masks. The review sheet includes circular and small-size checks.

The 16 px icon is supplied for technical use; the full three-letter mark is clearest at 32 px and above.

## Public copy

Use plain, direct sentences. Talk about the realm, the houses, the court, and the player's decisions. Use current build evidence for feature claims.

Suggested main line: **Keep the peace. Hold the crown.**
