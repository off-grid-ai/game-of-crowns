# Top 10: what the best game designers understand

Research only. Nothing in the repo was changed.
Tags: **NEW** = not in our engagement model yet. **DEEPENS** = sharpens a line we have.
Sourcing: **OPENED** = page read. **WEAK** = snippet, secondhand, or recalled.
Review evidence is thin: no frequency counts exist. "Strong" is our read of how often a theme recurs.

---

## 1. The player is the star, not the designer
- **They understand:** the fun has to come from the player's choices and the story they make, not from the game playing itself.
- **Example:** RimWorld. A colonist's death is *your* tragedy.
- **Who:** Sid Meier ("the player should have the fun", via Soren Johnson) [Sid's Rules](https://www.designer-notes.com/?p=119) OPENED. Tynan Sylvester, RimWorld is a story generator [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/video-how-i-rimworld-i-found-success-through-ridiculous-contrarian-design) OPENED. Lucas Pope [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/lucas-pope-on-making-games-out-of-restrictions/) OPENED. Will Wright [GDC 2005 report](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gdc-2005-report-the-future-of-content) OPENED. Miyazaki [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2011/11/12/afterwords-dark-souls) OPENED.
- **Players say:** King of Dragon Pass and RimWorld praised for "a saga of your own making" (WEAK, snippets).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "player is the hero". Every ending line should name something *he* did, never a random event.

## 2. Every choice has a cost, and no answer is always right
- **They understand:** a decision is only interesting if each option gives and takes, and the situation changes the answer.
- **Example:** Papers, Please. Every stamp helps one person and hurts another.
- **Who:** Meier, GDC 2012 [write-up](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gdc-2012-sid-meier-on-how-to-see-games-as-sets-of-interesting-decisions) OPENED. Pope, [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/lucas-pope-on-making-games-out-of-restrictions/) OPENED. Papers, Please analysis (a blog, not Pope) [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/what-games-can-learn-from-the-engagement-layers-of-papers-please) OPENED. Rosewater, fan notes, WEAK.
- **Players say:** Frostpunk and Suzerain praised for choices with no clean answer (WEAK). Some call Frostpunk's choices overrated (WEAK).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "interesting decisions". Test each petition: does it have a gain *and* a loss on both sides?

## 3. Show the cause. Answer every action at once.
- **They understand:** hidden logic makes the computer the star. Players need to see why things happened, and see something happen the moment they act.
- **Example:** RollerCoaster Tycoon guest thoughts ("I'm hungry", "too sick") tell you what to fix next.
- **Who:** Johnson on Meier [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/analysis-sid-meier-s-key-design-lessons) OPENED. Dan Cook, feedback every second [Lost Garden](https://lostgarden.com/2007/07/19/the-chemistry-of-game-design/) OPENED. Shamus Young on RCT [blog](https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=57871) OPENED.
- **Players say:** King of Dragon Pass faulted for "take it on trust" effects (WEAK).
- **For us:** we already have this (pillar 3). Keep it. Hide the future, show the past: name *why* a house moved.

## 4. Something is always almost finished
- **They understand:** short, medium and long goals overlap, so finishing one drops you into the next.
- **Example:** Civ. A tech, a wonder and a conquest are all nearly done, so "one more turn".
- **Who:** Civ design lead via 2K (WEAK, snippet). Johnson on turn-based pacing [Designer Notes](https://www.designer-notes.com/2010/02/) OPENED. Player thread [Straight Dope](https://boards.straightdope.com/t/why-is-sid-meiers-civilization-so-addictive/530268) OPENED.
- **Players say:** the strongest praise theme in our review pass.
- **For us:** DEEPENS "open loops". End every season with one visible loose thread. Keep three aims at three lengths.

## 5. Repetition kills. Know what loops and what doesn't.
- **They understand:** loops build mastery, one-off events burn out. A repeating pool breaks the story.
- **Example:** Reigns: Game of Thrones. 1,000+ cards, yet the same situations return early.
- **Who:** Dan Cook, [Loops and Arcs](https://lostgarden.com/2012/04/30/loops-and-arcs) OPENED. Reigns reviewer [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/reviews/review-reigns-game-of-thrones) OPENED. Reigns maker Alliot [Inverse](https://inverse.com/gaming/23117-reigns-francois-alliot-nerial-developer-interview) OPENED.
- **Players say:** top complaint across Reigns, Witcher Reigns and CK3 (STRONG for Reigns; CK3 WEAK).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "fresh every reign". At ~1 decision a minute we only have 15-60 per run, so each one must be drawn from the realm's state, not a pool. Cook's test: strip the named events. Does the court still play?

## 6. The middle and late game must change
- **They understand:** the game has to escalate and bring new aims, or it plateaus.
- **Example:** Stellaris and CK3 late game: "just wars", "once you're big it's easy".
- **Who:** Pardo, Blizzard core design [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts) OPENED (with a [contrary view](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts-a-contrary-view) OPENED). Warcraft II's steady difficulty climb (WEAK, magazine via MobyGames).
- **Players say:** late-game emptiness and snowball are STRONG complaints (CK3, Stellaris, Manor Lords), but our sources are mostly snippets (WEAK).
- **For us:** NEW. The royal ladder past six aims is the right fix. Check that act 3 asks different questions than act 1, not bigger numbers.

## 7. Pace by intensity: build, peak, release, and land the ending
- **They understand:** tension must rise and fall. People remember the peak and the end.
- **Example:** Left 4 Dead's Director: crescendo, then a safe room. RimWorld's storytellers do the same.
- **Who:** Valve's director [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_4_Dead) OPENED, secondary. Peak-end rule [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak%E2%80%93end_rule) OPENED (a rule of thumb, later studies mixed). Sylvester, RimWorld storyteller (WEAK).
- **For us:** NEW. Add a director that picks each season's heat from the last: quiet after a crisis. Spend the most craft on the ending ("history names you").

## 8. Know the one feeling you're building, and cut the rest
- **They understand:** pick the emotion first. A second game inside the first undermines it.
- **Example:** Pirates! and Civ stayed one-focus. Covert Action's sub-games fought each other.
- **Who:** Meier/Johnson (Covert Action rule) [Sid's Rules](https://www.designer-notes.com/?p=119) OPENED. Pope, Sylvester (strategic omission) OPENED. Rosewater "restrictions breed creativity", fan notes WEAK. MDA paper (WEAK, recalled).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "the promise". If the siege becomes its own tactics game, cut it. Everything must answer "what kind of king am I?"

## 9. Change big, test with real players, and cut
- **They understand:** small tweaks teach nothing. Double or halve it. Cut a third of your ideas. Players spot problems but design bad fixes.
- **Example:** Dark Souls started very hard and was eased down after playtests said "easier".
- **Who:** Meier/Johnson OPENED. Miyazaki [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2011/11/12/afterwords-dark-souls) OPENED. Blow on Braid [MCV](https://mcvuk.com/development-news/blow-braid-designed-itself) OPENED. Pardo (tuning is easy to do, hard to do well) OPENED.
- **For us:** NEW to our rules. Seasonal courts give few tests per hour, so tune with big swings. Take the problem from players, not the fix.

## 10. People who remember specific acts, not a number
- **They understand:** a single favour bar lets small gifts wash out a massacre. Characters must remember *what you did*.
- **Example:** Shadow of Mordor's nemesis system (our example, WEAK). Fallout 3 karma is the warning.
- **Who:** Critique of Levine's "narrative lego" [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/stepped-on-a-narrative-lego) OPENED; the talk itself is [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1020211) (WEAK, snippet). Sylvester on attachment via logs (WEAK).
- **Players say:** emergent personal stories from characters are STRONG praise (RimWorld, CK3, KoDP).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "my choices come back". Let houses hold grudges for named acts ("the broken oath at Kingsmere"), and scheme against *each other*.

## Also-rans (real, but already in our doc or weaker)
- Fun is learning; easy to learn, deep to master. Koster, Cook, Pardo. Already in our model.
- Surprise and discovery. Miyazaki OPENED, Schell/Miyamoto not verified. Mostly fits action games.
- Failure is content (Dwarf Fortress, Reigns' dynasty). Tarn Adams quotes WEAK. Worth a look: a deposed king should still be a good ending.
- Quantic Foundry motivations: a ruler game fits Strategy, Power, Story, Fantasy. WEAK.
- UI overload punished (Civ VII). STRONG for Civ VII only. Matches our "one thing at a time".
- Juice (Jonasson & Purho) and flow (Jenova Chen): pages did not load. WEAK.
