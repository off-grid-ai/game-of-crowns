# Platform sizes and placement

Checked: 4 November 2026.

## X

The header export is 1500 × 500 px. The profile export is 400 × 400 px. Keep key details away from the outer edges and the lower-left profile-photo area. X notes that header crops can change with the display. [X profile guide](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/how-to-customize-your-profile), [X upload and crop notes](https://help.x.com/en/managing-your-account/common-issues-when-uploading-profile-photo.html).

## YouTube

The banner export is 2560 × 1440 px and is below 6 MB. Important title content sits within the centred 1544 × 420 px area. This is a conservative scaled version of YouTube's stated 1235 × 338 px text area at 2048 × 1152 px. YouTube crops the banner for each display. [YouTube channel branding guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en).

A 1280 × 720 px thumbnail image and a 150 × 150 px transparent watermark are also supplied. The thumbnail is promotional artwork; use it where that description is accurate.

## Discord

The banner is 960 × 540 px, with no title text. Discord recommends that size, a quiet top edge, and banner artwork without a logo or text. [Discord server banner guide](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028716472-Server-Banners).

These are prepared sizes, not a claim of store approval or a confirmed Steam release. Use the store's own preview before you upload.

## General social formats

The kit also contains a 1080 px square, a 1080 × 1350 px portrait, a 1080 × 1920 px story image, a 1920 × 1080 px landscape image, and a 1200 × 630 px link preview. These are reusable format choices. The receiving site can apply its own crop.
