# Go-to-market (2026-11-05)

Built from brief.md, angle.md, and three research sweeps: small-team breakouts, channel data, and big-studio and AI-game launches. Numbers are from GameDiscoverCo, howtomarketagame, press and postmortems; sources at the end.

## What actually moves a small game
1. **A playable demo, then Steam Next Fest.** Manor Lords got ~500k wishlists in its Next Fest week; Thronefall and Bongo Cat broke out off fest demos. A demo up months before the fest earns ~2.5x the median. Going in with 1k+ wishlists gets a median ~1,000 from the fest; under 1k gets ~320.
2. **One creator video.** Seeth for Songs of Syx ("6 months' revenue in 24h"), Let's Game It Out for WorldBox (10M+ views), DruuuWu for Ages of Conflict (1.8M, off a free itch.io build), CaseOh for Suck Up! and Schedule I. Suck Up! got there with no budget: cold emails and DMs to mid-size streamers, then the big ones followed within two weeks.
3. **Steam itself.** Its algorithm and friends of wishlisters bring about half of all wishlists; 63% of Dwarf Fortress's launch traffic was Steam's own. Outside marketing works mostly by tripping Steam's algorithm.
4. **Other people's short videos** (TikTok, Shorts, Reels): ~35% of one publisher's wishlists, peaking around announcements and demos.
5. **Reddit gameplay clips:** 0–150 wishlists in niche subs; big subs are a lottery (one 30s clip on r/gaming: 11k in a day). Reddit ads now work at ~$1–2 a wishlist; stop above $3.

**Overrated:** press (writes about you once you're already popular), X and Bluesky (~50–70 wishlists for a viral post), our own social posts (~5%), paid showcase slots ($250k+), Discord as a way to find people (it keeps people; it doesn't find them).

## What's special about us
- **World sims spread by being watched.** "I let it run for 1,000 years" and "AI nations battle" fill a video by themselves. Creators pick these games because the game makes the story.
- **AI moments are the clip.** Where Winds Meet and Suck Up! spread because players filmed what the AI characters said and did. Our clip-sized moment: *the lord remembered what I did to his brother.* A WorldBox timelapse can't show that; we can.
- **The AI label costs on Steam:** 31% of Steam players view AI disclosure negatively and 8% won't buy. Free removes "won't buy"; leading with the game, not "AI", handles the rest. r/LocalLLaMA and Hacker News are the exceptions, where "every lord runs on a local model, offline" is the headline.
- **Free:** discovered through Steam's Free to Play hub, New & Trending and free-game aggregators. Steam players distrust free-to-play, so say it plainly: free, no microtransactions, nothing locked.
- **Off Grid AI:** the game shows what on-device AI can do. Tech demos alone fade (Inworld Origins was delisted); a real game that Off Grid can point its own users to doesn't.

## The plan
**Now, while the game catches up**
- Reddit discussion posts (posts.md) to test the angle and collect what players want; comments in WorldBox, Ages of Conflict and map-sim threads.
- A weekly 20–40s clip, posted to Shorts, TikTok and the subs that allow it: one realm event and its cause per clip. Each one is also the Steam page's future GIF.
- A Discord, only as a home for whoever shows up.

**Steam page and free demo (needs the art licences cleared first)**
- Steam page live 6–12 months before launch.
- A free demo, a short reign whose save carries into the full game, live months before the target Next Fest (they run Feb, June and Oct; June 2027 is realistic).
- Cold-DM 30 mid-size creators who play WorldBox, Ages of Conflict, map sims and Manor Lords, with the demo and one clip.
- "Show HN" and r/LocalLLaMA: the on-device minds.
- A $300 Reddit ads test; keep it only under $3 a wishlist.

**Next Fest, then launch within six months.** Target 1k+ wishlists going in.

## What to measure
Replies with substance per post; follows and wishlists; demo players; creators who play it unprompted after a DM. Stop doing whatever brings nothing after two tries.

## Build asks sent to the main session (2026-11-05)
Already sent: a lord remembering on screen, faster years, the chronicle naming causes, a chain clip.
New from this research:
1. **A watch mode made for video:** let the realm run fast, the camera going to events with chronicle captions, and a one-click 30s clip export. Creators and our weekly clips both need it.
2. **A free demo build:** a short reign whose save carries into the full game.
3. **Art licences** cleared for public release (VISION: "personal use only"). This blocks a Steam page and a demo.
