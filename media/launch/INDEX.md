# Launch media kit

4K stills and short clips of Aldmere for the launch video, every shot a low
oblique look across the land. Rebuild with `realm/tools/media.sh [stills|clips|intro|all] [name ...]`
(renders queue through `realm/tools/render.sh`; clips are Movie Maker takes from
`realm/scripts/cinematics.gd`). The files themselves are gitignored; this list says what each shows.
