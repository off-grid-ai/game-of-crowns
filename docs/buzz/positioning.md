# Positioning: how the hits say it (Steam short descriptions, read 2026-11-05)

| Game | Genre first | The promise |
|---|---|---|
| WorldBox | "god simulator and sandbox" | "Watch civilizations grow… empires to fall and towns to burn!" |
| RimWorld | "sci-fi colony sim" | "driven by an intelligent AI storyteller. Generates stories…" |
| Songs of Syx | "fantasy city-builder" | "tiny events can spiral into the collapse of kingdoms" |
| Crusader Kings III | "grand strategy" | "Love, fight, scheme, and claim greatness." |
| Dwarf Fortress | fortress builder | "the deepest, most intricate simulation of a world" |
| Fantasy Map Simulator | map simulation | "watch imaginary worlds evolve over centuries" |
| Manor Lords | "medieval strategy game" | "the seasons pass… and cities rise and fall" |
| Norland | "medieval colony sim" | "class conflict, religious struggle, and political treachery" |
| inZOI | life sim | "Every life becomes a story" |

## What they share
1. **Genre in the first words.** The reader knows what kind of game it is before the hook.
2. **One promise, in drama verbs:** burn, fall, scheme, spiral. No features.
3. **The story is the product.** RimWorld, inZOI and Songs of Syx sell the stories the sim makes, not the sim.
4. **Songs of Syx already says Mac's hook:** small events spiral into the fall of kingdoms. The itch is real and sells; we need our own angle on it.

## Our angle: Game of Thrones x Westworld
- **Game of Thrones** = great houses, politics, betrayal, a King who can't just order everyone around. Everyone gets it in one second.
- **Westworld** = the people aren't scenery; they remember, want things and act on their own.
- Together: **a medieval realm whose lords have minds of their own, and you're the King who has to rule them.** Nobody in the table above says that. CK3 is closest, but there you play the schemer; here the schemers are everyone else.

## Two risks
- **"AI" in the pitch.** A study of 508k Steam reviews found games that disclose generative AI get lower recommendation rates; players read it as a shortcut ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11539v1)). So sell what the player feels ("every lord remembers how you ruled"), never "LLM NPCs". Answer honestly when asked: a local model gives the characters their voices. r/LocalLLaMA is the one place to lead with it.
- **The names.** "Game of Thrones x Westworld" is a great way to explain it in a comment or a DM, but in a title or store page it invites trademark replies. In titles, describe it instead.

## Lines to test
1. **"A medieval kingdom that runs itself. You're the King, and every lord remembers how you ruled."** (genre + the Westworld half, plainly)
2. **"Six great houses scheme, starve and go to war without you. You're the King. Choose who to back."** (drama verbs, the Thrones half)
3. In comments: **"Think Game of Thrones politics, but the lords are Westworld hosts: they remember you and act on their own."**
