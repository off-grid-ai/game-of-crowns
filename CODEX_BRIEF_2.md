## 1. Buildings we're missing (Sketchfab, free, downloadable, glTF)

- **A blacksmith's forge or smithy** (open-fronted workshop with anvil and chimney). Under ~60k faces.

## 2. Game-ready trees (the important one)
The Poly Haven trees are film scans (2–17 million triangles each) and can't be used for forests. We need **game-ready** trees: **under ~15k triangles each**, ideally with LODs or a billboard.
- Species: **European beech, oak, birch, Scots pine, Norway spruce**, plus a few **bushes** and a **dead tree**.
- Best source: **Fab (Epic) Quixel Megascans** trees, which are free with an Epic login and come with LODs. Download the low-poly LOD (LOD1–LOD3) versions as FBX or glTF.
- Otherwise: Sketchfab game-ready trees in a realistic style, under the face budget.
- List in the report each tree's triangle count and whether it has LODs or a billboard.
