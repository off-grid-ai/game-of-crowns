# Mobile mockups

Six screens of Game of Crowns on a landscape phone, drawn with the Codex CLI image tool (2026-11-20) from the in-game frames and the North Star art. See docs/ANDROID.md for the design behind them.

## Shared style prompt

```
Make ONE image with your image generation tool, 1536x1024 landscape. It shows a modern Android phone held in landscape, seen straight on, thin black bezel, filling most of the canvas on a plain dark charcoal background. The phone's 20:9 screen shows a mobile game screenshot from "Game of Crowns", a medieval war-king strategy game set in the realm of Aldmere, about 900 AD (Viking and Anglo-Saxon age: round shields, nasal helms, mail shirts, spears, Dane axes, bows, wool cloaks, furs; never plate armour or late-medieval dress). Look: a real-time 3D strategy game, stylised painted textures, muted grounded autumn palette: weathered red-brown roofs, white-washed and grey stone walls, olive-grey ground, autumn trees, soft low sunlight. Muted house colours, never bright saturated red; not playful or cartoonish. Camera is a low oblique angle (about 35-50 degrees), never top-down. The attached images are the game's own look (in-game frames and approved concept art): match their buildings, people and colour grade. UI: a modern premium mobile strategy HUD in the style of Rome: Total War mobile and Rise of Kingdoms: dark translucent panels with thin bronze edges, white text, big thumb-sized buttons (at least 48dp), everything inside the safe area, the bottom corners used for actions. Every state is shown in WHITE WORDS or shapes, never colour alone. The rightmost button is the primary one. Spell all UI text exactly as given, nothing else written. 
```

## 1-realm.jpg

SCREEN: the realm map. Low oblique view of the realm of Aldmere in autumn: a river delta and coast, hills, forests, small walled towns, and seven house seats each with a tall muted banner. Kingsmere, the royal town, sits on the river in the middle. Top-left a dark pill: "Drive the raiders from Ashford". Top-centre four icon+number pairs: a crown "1,240", a grain sack "820", a helmet "3,100", and a laurel "the Untested". Left edge: a column of three round commander portraits (bearded men in mail), each with a troop count beneath ("1,200", "640", "300"). Bottom-centre: a row of seven small heraldic shields, each labelled below in white: "Ally", "Foe", "Waits", "Waits", "Ally", "Foe", "Waits". Right edge: a red-brown wax-seal button with "1 matter" beside it, and a pause button below it.

## 2-town.jpg

SCREEN: Kingsmere town, zoomed to roof height. Dense medieval town: weathered red-brown roofs, white-washed timber houses, a grey stone keep, a market square with townsfolk, olive ground and autumn trees. The royal host stands in a neat block formation by the town gate under the King's banner. Top-left a dark pill: "Kingsmere · the Crown's · Grain 420". Bottom-centre a wide dark card with three choices side by side, each with a painted icon and a price: "Archers · 200", "Stone wall · 300", "Granary · 150". Right edge: the wax-seal button and a pause button.

## 3-battle.jpg

SCREEN: a field battle. Low oblique view of two dense hosts on an autumn field: the King's mail-clad spearmen in a tight shield wall of round shields facing an enemy wedge of axemen in muted grey-blue (House Wolfholt), archers behind both lines, dust in the air. A drawn white line in front of the King's troops shows a new frontage, with a fingertip touching it and the words "Shield wall" under the finger. Top-centre: two strength bars labelled "Crown 1,840" and "Wolfholt 1,520". Left edge: a column of five small troop cards, each with a soldier portrait, a count and a white morale word ("Steady", "Steady", "Shaken", "Steady", "Eager"). Bottom-centre a command bar of five buttons with icons and labels: "Line", "Shield wall", "Wedge", "Hold", "Charge". Right edge: "Pause" and speed "-" "+".

## 4-siege.jpg

SCREEN: a siege. A grey stone town wall with a cracked section and a breach, a wooden ram under a hide roof at the gate, archers on the wall, smoke rising from burning roofs inside. The King's host is camped outside with tents and muted banners. Top-left a dark banner: "War on Wolfholt · 2 of 6 towns · winning". Bottom-centre a dark card with three big choices: "Starve · 8 days", "Build rams", "Assault"; the rightmost, "Assault", has a bright bronze frame and the word "Ready". Right edge: a pause button.

## 5-court.jpg

SCREEN: a court decision. The 3D world (Kingsmere's roofs) stays visible but dimmed behind a central parchment card. The card shows a painted portrait of a grey-bearded lord in mail and a fur cloak with a gold-crest brooch, his name "Lord Aldric of Goldcrest", and two lines of his words in a serif font: "My men bled at the fords for you. Give us the river lands, as you swore." Below the portrait two small house shields with white text "Goldcrest +10" and "Stormhold -10". At the card's bottom two large buttons: "Refuse" on the left (plain), "Grant the land" on the right (primary, bronze). A ring top-right of the card with "2 seasons".

## 6-title.jpg

SCREEN: the title screen. Kingsmere at golden-hour autumn from a low oblique angle, the King's host marching out of the town gate in a long column under muted banners, river and hills behind. "GAME OF CROWNS" in a heavy carved stone serif across the upper third. Bottom-left two small text buttons "Settings" and "Continue"; bottom-right one large bronze button "Begin your reign". No other UI.
