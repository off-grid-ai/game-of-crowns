# Top 10: what makes a game people stream and clip

Research only. Nothing in the game was changed.
Tags: **NEW** = not in GAME_DESIGN.md yet. **DEEPENS** = sharpens a line we have.
Sourcing: **OPENED** = page read. **WEAK** = snippet, secondhand, or recalled. **EST** = a tracker's estimate, not Twitch's own count.
Viewer numbers come from third-party trackers (Streams Charts, SteamDB, TwitchTracker). They disagree with each other. Treat every one as EST.

---

## 1. A viewer who just arrived can read it in seconds
- **They understand:** most viewers never did the tutorial. The goal, the stakes and the score must be on screen, always.
- **Example:** Slay the Spire. Fans built "Slay the Relics", a Twitch overlay so viewers can hover a relic and read it. The game itself was hard to read on stream.
- **Who:** Day[9], "game summary information" [GameDev](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-make-your-games-better-to-stream-according-to-day-9-) OPENED. InCuDe study of 257 stream viewers: interface comes first [UCLan](https://clok.uclan.ac.uk/33166) WEAK (abstract only). Slay the Relics / Spire Scryer [STR mod](https://github.com/Spireblight/STR-Spire-Mod) WEAK.
- **Numbers:** none that isolate readability.
- **For us:** DEEPENS "I can read the board". Test at stream size (720p, chat panel covering the right third): is the petition, the name you're earning and the coming threat readable? A newcomer should say "he's a king, that house is about to rise" within 10 s.

## 2. Viewers shout "I'd do it differently" before the choice
- **They understand:** the best stream moment is the pause before a hard choice. Chat argues, the streamer decides, everyone reacts.
- **Example:** Papers, Please. Every stamp is a small moral test the audience can judge. Reigns: one card, two answers.
- **Who:** Day[9]: "What would I do differently?" and "mind mechanics", where viewers predict the move [GameDev](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-make-your-games-better-to-stream-according-to-day-9-) OPENED. Twitch Plays design lessons [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/twitch-plays-everything-designing-for-a-new-frontier) OPENED.
- **Numbers:** PewDiePie's Papers, Please video ~6.5M views (WEAK, 2019 secondary source).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "every answer costs". We already have the right shape: the world pauses while court sits. Keep the card up until the King answers (no timer forcing him). Both sides of each answer must be visible to chat, not only to the player.

## 3. Chat plays the crowd on slow, big decisions
- **They understand:** stream delay is 20 s or more, so chat can't steer in real time. Let it vote on big choices with long windows, and let the streamer overrule.
- **Example:** Reigns' own Twitch Plays (2016): chat votes on each card, the card tilts toward the winning side, the streamer can take over at any time. Dead Cells: chat picks upgrades and plays the healing bird.
- **Who:** Reigns Twitch Plays [press release](https://cosmocover.com/newsroom/reigns-twitch-plays) OPENED. Dead Cells, Motion Twin [Twitch blog](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2019/01/07/how-twitch-integration-doubled-all-time-minutes-watched-in-a-month-4cdddaeab17c) OPENED and [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/streamer-modes-are-usually-gimmicky-bs-but-in-dead-cells-its-magic) OPENED. Choice Chamber: polls up to 60 s, "you can't have precision interaction" [GameDev](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/twitch-plays-everything-designing-for-a-new-frontier) OPENED. Jackbox extended timers for stream delay [Jackbox](https://www.jackboxgames.com/streaming-moderation-accessibility-features-jackbox-party-pack-eight/) OPENED and [Destructoid](https://destructoid.com/?p=161428) OPENED. Slay the Spire chat votes on cards and relics; Vampire Survivors Twitch Mode for level-ups (WEAK, snippets).
- **Numbers:** Dead Cells' Twitch integration "pretty much doubled our all-time minutes watched count in a month" (developer claim, OPENED). Jackbox saw streamers pull "100 or more people" into one game (OPENED). Crowd Control says 175+ games use its effects (vendor claim, WEAK).
- **Warning:** Choice Chamber found chat punishes a streamer who ignores it (OPENED). Steam users found Dead Cells' chat spam disruptive (WEAK). The streamer must stay in charge.
- **For us:** NEW. "The realm speaks": with Twitch linked, chat votes on the open petition. The card leans toward the crowd's answer, like Reigns. The King can follow the crowd or overrule it, and overruling is itself a choice the chronicle remembers ("the King ignored his people"). The court already pauses the world, so a 30-60 s vote costs nothing. Feedback lives on the card (a lean and a count), never a new panel.

## 4. The game makes stories nobody wrote
- **They understand:** a stream is worth watching when nobody knows what happens next, not even the streamer. Systems that collide make stories worth retelling.
- **Example:** Crusader Kings 2 spread through player stories, not ads: "the crazy situations that arise out of the game". Among Us: every round is a new whodunit.
- **Who:** Henrik Fåhraeus, Paradox [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/crusader-kings-2/how-crusader-kings-2-caught-paradox-by-surprise) OPENED. He wished he'd spent more time on emergent story. Among Us growth via streamer groups [Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/phenomena-2020-among-us) OPENED. RimWorld as a story generator (see designer-top10.md, OPENED there).
- **Numbers:** Among Us 2020: 147M hours watched in September, 774K peak viewers in November (EST, OPENED). Crusader Kings 3 Twitch peak 64,778 (EST, SteamDB snippet, WEAK). Dwarf Fortress Steam launch Twitch peak 40.2K, 7 Dec 2022 (EST, WEAK).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "a story I'd retell" and "my choices come back". Houses scheming against *each other*, grudges for named acts, and lords whose deeds play where they happen are exactly what a stream needs. Guard against scripted beats that play the same for every streamer.

## 5. Failure is funny or dramatic, and it's the streamer's fault
- **They understand:** losing is content. A loud, fair fall gets clipped. A quiet "game over" menu does not.
- **Example:** Lethal Company: proximity voice cuts off the moment a friend dies, "terrifying and hilarious". Getting Over It: one slip, all progress gone. Manor Lords: Gronkh lost two oxen in a fire and stopped his stream.
- **Who:** Lethal Company reviews [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/steams-new-viral-co-op-horror-hit-has-30000-overwhelmingly-positive-reviews-and-100000-peak-players-rivaling-call-of-duty-pubg-and-apex-legends) WEAK (snippet). Getting Over It and streamers [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/we-made-the-developer-of-getting-over-it-play-his-own-i-1821193180) WEAK; whether Foddy designed for streams is unproven. Manor Lords streamers [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/manor-lords-twitch-gronkh-maurice,1116497) OPENED.
- **Numbers:** Lethal Company Steam peak 240,817 players (WEAK, SteamDB snippet). Manor Lords Twitch peak 66.2K on launch day, 26 Apr 2024 (EST, WEAK). Manor Lords also shows the cost: Maurice Weber drifted off when the late game ran thin (OPENED).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "failure feels fair". Being Deposed must be the best scene in the game: the banners turn, the crowd at the gate, the lord who did it named. It plays after the ultimatum, so the streamer knows it was his doing. A deposed king is still a good stream ending.

## 6. The streamer's character shows through the game
- **They understand:** people watch a person, not a game. The best games let the streamer's taste, morals and humour drive what happens.
- **Example:** Dead by Daylight streamers split into the skilled and the joking, both on the same game. Papers, Please videos are judged on how cruel the streamer was. Suck Up!: players talk to AI characters in their own voice, and the bits went viral.
- **Who:** Dead by Daylight streamer styles [ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=35924) WEAK. Suck Up! spread [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/subcultures/suck-up) OPENED.
- **Numbers:** Suck Up!: MadMorph's Let's Play passed 1.1M views in a month; one TikTok clip passed 1.7M in three weeks (OPENED, page counts).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "the promise". "Find out what kind of king you'd be" is already a streamer's premise: the audience watches to find out what kind of king *he* is. The name he's earning, live on screen, is the running joke and the cliffhanger. Talking to a lord in his own voice is a natural clip; show what he said and what the lord answered as large captions. The game still plays fully without the model.

## 7. Viewers become people in the game
- **They understand:** a viewer whose name is on a character watches to see it live or die.
- **Example:** Marbles on Stream: type `!play` and a marble with your name races. RimWorld mods let viewers buy a named colonist. Dead Cells: one viewer plays the healing bird.
- **Who:** Marbles on Stream [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1170970/discussions/0/4516632983095949848) WEAK. RimWorld Twitch Toolkit and Interactive Chat Services (mods, not Ludeon) [GitHub](https://gh.ali360.cloudns.ch/ekudram/-cap-RimworldInteractiveChatServices) WEAK. Dead Cells [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/streamer-modes-are-usually-gimmicky-bs-but-in-dead-cells-its-magic) OPENED.
- **Numbers:** Marbles on Stream ~1.9M hours watched in June 2026 (EST, WEAK), for a game that is nearly all chat.
- **For us:** NEW. "Chat names the lord": with Twitch linked, petitioners and minor lords and heirs take names from chat (filtered). They come back at later courts, so the viewer waits for his turn. Fits "people I care about" and "no two share a name".

## 8. Moments that stand alone in 30 seconds
- **They understand:** YouTube and TikTok spread clips, not streams. A clip must make sense with no context: setup, choice, payoff.
- **Example:** Vampire Survivors grew from one YouTube video. Suck Up! grew on TikTok clips. Balatro: the hand that scores a billion.
- **Who:** Day[9], "a two- or three-minute feat" you can record [GameDev](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-make-your-games-better-to-stream-according-to-day-9-) OPENED. Vampire Survivors growth [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-vampire-survivors-went-from-obscurity-to-27000-people-playing-at-once) OPENED. Balatro word of mouth [Push to Talk](https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/the-word-of-mouth-magic-behind-balatro) OPENED.
- **Numbers:** Vampire Survivors went from a peak of 14 players to 1,143 the day SplatterCatGaming posted a 30-minute video, then 27,000+ (OPENED). Balatro: 48 wishlists in May 2023, 28,661 after streamer Dan Gheesling's June stream and July, 208,401 at launch (OPENED). Twitch made 124M clips in 2017 (WEAK).
- **For us:** NEW. Each act needs one peak that reads in 30 s with no context: a betrayal at the gate, an execution, a feast turned to a brawl. The camera already goes to the biggest result; make sure that shot has a caption naming who and why, and that it holds long enough to clip.

## 9. Sessions come in chunks with natural breaks
- **They understand:** streamers need pauses to talk to chat, and YouTubers need episodes. A run with a clear start and end is easy to stream and easy to edit.
- **Example:** Balatro and Slay the Spire runs. Jackbox rounds. Among Us games of a few minutes.
- **Who:** Day[9]: "Is there a way to record just a chapter?" [GameDev](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-make-your-games-better-to-stream-according-to-day-9-) OPENED. TwitchCon recap: streamers favour short, separable sessions with breaks to talk to chat [Triple Point PR](https://blog.triplepointpr.com/?p=7537) WEAK.
- **Numbers:** none found.
- **For us:** DEEPENS "a reign has a shape". 15/30/60-minute reigns are already the right size: a 30-minute reign is one YouTube episode, a 60-minute one is one stream segment. The court is the break to read chat. The four act interludes are chapter marks. Don't add endless reigns.

## 10. The ending is something you share
- **They understand:** a short, spoiler-free result card spreads on its own and starts "how did you do?" talk.
- **Example:** Wordle's emoji grid. A player typed it out by hand; Wardle built it in. It tells your story without spoiling the answer.
- **Who:** Josh Wardle, GDC 2022 [GameDev](https://gamedeveloper.com/gdc2022/josh-wardle-reflects-on-the-the-unconventional-road-to-wordle-s-success) OPENED. Shared seeds and daily runs in roguelikes (WEAK, recalled).
- **Numbers:** none with a clean source. Wordle sold for "low seven figures" (OPENED).
- **For us:** DEEPENS "end of the reign" in the feedback ladder. The ending screen should export one image: "Aldric the Bloody", the realm map in house colours, three lines on what he did, and the realm's seed. Another streamer can play the same Aldmere and compare names. Not a stats dashboard.

## Also-rans (real, but weaker for us or already covered)
- **Streamer-safe settings.** Jackbox: hide room codes, profanity and family filters, extended timers (OPENED). Licensed music gets VODs muted; streamer modes exist for that (Twitch DMCA notes, WEAK). For us: all our music must be ours or CC0; any chat name passes a filter.
- **Group play with friends** (Among Us, Lethal Company, Peak) drove the biggest spikes, but needs multiplayer. Out of scope.
- **The developer joins the stream.** Peak's devs played the toy Bingbong live in streamers' games (WEAK). Funny, not a design rule.
- **Discovery is word of mouth first.** Balatro's streamer found it in his own Discord, not by email; he gets 10-25 keys a week (OPENED). Streamers amplify; they don't start it.
- **Thin late game loses streamers.** Manor Lords' top German streamer drifted off (OPENED). Already item 6 of designer-top10.md.

## What needs to be done (by impact)
1. **Read-at-stream-size pass.** Petition, name being earned and coming threat readable at 720p with chat covering the right third. Test with someone who has never played.
2. **"The realm speaks" (chat votes the petition)** via Twitcher. The card leans toward chat's answer; the King follows or overrules, and the chronicle remembers which. 30-60 s window while court sits.
3. **Make the fall the best scene.** Deposed and Bloody endings play as a short, captioned scene naming the lord who did it.
4. **One clippable peak per act.** The biggest-result shot holds long enough and carries a caption naming who and why.
5. **Chat names the lord.** Petitioners, heirs and minor lords take filtered names from chat and return at later courts.
6. **Shareable ending image plus realm seed.** One card: the name, the map, three lines. Spoiler-free.
7. **Streamer mode.** Our own or CC0 music only, name filter, no forced timers, hides nothing the player needs.
8. **Voice talk as captions.** When the King speaks to a lord, show both lines large enough to read on stream.

## Where sourcing is weak
- No study measures *why* a game gets clipped. Traits come from developer statements, Day[9]'s talk, and growth stories. The ranking is our judgment.
- Viewer numbers are third-party estimates. Streams Charts blocked us (403); its figures here come from search snippets. SteamDB and TwitchTracker were not opened.
- Not opened: GDC Vault "Built for Broadcasting" and "Building Interactive Streams" (members only), the InCuDe paper's full ten heuristics, the Slay the Spire and Vampire Survivors Twitch modes' own notes, any Ludeon statement on streaming.
- No streaming numbers found for Reigns, Suzerain, Frostpunk, King of Dragon Pass or Kingdom. Our closest genre is under-measured.
- Peak and Lethal Company "why" is from reviews and press, not the developers.
- Getting Over It: no proof Foddy designed it for streams.
- Twitch poll eligibility (Affiliate/Partner) not checked against Twitch's docs.

## The honest read for Mac
- Our shape already suits streams: a pause before each choice, 15-60 minute runs, a name at the end.
- The two NEW things that would matter most: chat votes on the petition (Reigns did it in 2016) and chat names the lords.
- A proven MIT block on Godot 4.7 exists for Twitch (Twitcher). None exists for YouTube chat.
