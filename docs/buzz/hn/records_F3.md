## Cross-cutting notes (from these 5)
- Three of the five had no post text at all. The title and an instant-play link carried them. The two with posts (Time Portal, Reconquer) used one to state the stack and numbers, and Time Portal also stated the flaws up front.
- Reply speed did not predict points here (Whatsit had 6 replies starting at 3 h and still got 513), but it shaped the tone: Time Portal's 66 gracious replies kept the AI criticism civil, and Counter-Strike's flippant replies drew rebukes.
- Each thread fixated on one avoidable issue: the US-sports first puzzle, AI anachronisms, the missing license, server/SMTP capacity, or disputed answer labels. Expect HN to find the one weak spot; fixing it live is rewarded.
- For an AI-built or AI-content game: name the flaw in the post, concede quickly, frame the AI as what makes the game possible ("removes the constraints"), and don't promise that future models will fix it.
