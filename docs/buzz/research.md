# How other devs gauge interest on Reddit (researched 2026-11-05)

## What works
- **A short clip beats everything.** Under 60 seconds, uploaded to Reddit directly. In the first seconds: the genre, the hook, the player doing something, a threat. Text-only posts and screenshots without context underperform. ([presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/reddit-marketing-indie-games), [howtomarketagame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/01/10/how-a-two-person-team-earned-11000-wishlists-with-one-reddit-post/))
- **Genre subs convert better than big general ones**, because the readers already like the genre. r/gamedev gets attention but few players. (presskit.gg)
- **Context earns replies.** The same GIF got 12 upvotes in one sub and 400 in another after a paragraph explaining the problem was added. ([polylusion](https://polylusion-games-site.pages.dev/blog/indie-game-dev-subreddit))
- **Write like a person.** Personal-stakes titles ("I spent a year…") still work, but only if the game holds up. (howtomarketagame)

## What gets you removed or ignored
- A promotional post dressed up as a genuine question. Say you're the dev up front. (presskit.gg)
- A new or empty account. Moderators check profiles; the 11k-wishlist dev had about 11 non-promotional posts first. (howtomarketagame)
- The same post in several subs at once. Space them out: one post per sub per month, and at most one a week for the project overall.
- Links to your own game in subs that ban them (r/indiegames feedback posts: no links).

## What counts as interest
- Upvotes are a weak signal: "2,000 upvotes doesn't mean a viable game." (polylusion)
- Stronger signals: people following somewhere (Steam wishlist or playtest sign-up, Discord, subreddit), people asking where to play, people telling their own stories or arguing the design.
- A check that needs no posting: on SteamDB, look at follower counts of 5–10 comparable games to size the audience. ([immutable](https://www.immutable.com/guides/how-to-market-an-indie-game))

## What this means for us
1. Our first post failed on the known causes: text only, a title that named a genre, no question.
2. The itch post (#1) works as text, since it's a real discussion, with the disclosure in its first line.
3. The show post (#3) needs a clip of one real chain, not screenshots.
4. We need somewhere to follow before #3. A Steam page with playtest sign-ups is the strongest signal but needs the licence question settled. Discord or a subreddit are free today.
