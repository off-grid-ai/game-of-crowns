# Downloads first (2026-11-05)

Mac's focus: get the game into people's hands. Everything else (community, Discord, daily realms) waits until people are playing. community.md and virality.md are for later.

## What has to be true before anyone can download
1. **A signed, notarised Mac dmg.** Today's build is ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper blocks it on every other Mac (docs/SHIPPING.md). The Developer ID secrets are on the Off Grid AI Desktop repo.
2. **A download page that counts.** A GitHub Release for the dmg, plus a free itch.io page: itch shows downloads by where people came from, and its Free, Simulation and Strategy browse pages bring their own traffic.
3. **The size said up front:** the app plus a 1.8 GB model fetched on first run.
4. **A 20–30s clip** at the top of every post and page: a lord bringing up what you did to him, and acting on it.
5. **The first 5 minutes show the hook:** the model download starts at once with a progress line, and the first reign shows a lord remembering, by rules if the model isn't ready yet.

## Where downloads come from (Mac only for now, ~2% of Steam players)
Ranked by fit with a free, native Apple Silicon game:
1. **r/macgaming** (~209k). Mac gamers get few native games, let alone free ones. *Free and native on Apple Silicon: a kingdom sim where every lord remembers what you did.*
2. **Mac gaming creators:** Andrew Tsai (~109k subscribers, covers native Mac games), then smaller Mac channels. One DM each with the clip and the link.
3. **itch.io's own browse pages** (free, simulation, strategy, macOS): tagged right, they bring downloads every day without us posting.
4. **r/SimulationGames, r/4Xgaming:** the clip-led post (posts.md).
5. **Comments** where people ask for games like WorldBox, Ages of Conflict or map sims, with the link.
6. **r/godot:** how it's built.
7. **Mac game lists:** AppleGamingWiki and the MacRumors gaming threads.

A post that flops costs one try; the next channel is independent of it. Space them out (one post per sub, a few days apart) and reuse the same clip.

**Then a Windows build**, which opens the other ~95%: r/indiegames, gaming creators, a free Steam page.

## What to measure
Downloads per channel (itch referrers, GitHub release counts) each week. Keep what brings downloads; drop what brings nothing after two tries.

## Asks of the main session, in this order
1. Signed and notarised Mac release, published as a GitHub Release.
2. The first-run hook (model download at once; a lord remembering in the first reign).
3. A lord remembering on screen, and a 20–30s clip of it.
4. A Windows build.
5. Later: faster years, the saga card, friends in the realm, the community realm.
