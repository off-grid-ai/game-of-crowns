Horse based on "Horse Rigged All Gaits" by fdoss001 (https://blendswap.com/blend/28627), using the base "Horse" by Tarnyloo (https://blendswap.com/blend/17172). Source labels: CC0 for the All Gaits contribution and CC BY for the base model.
Modified for Game of Crowns: reduced to 38,942 triangles, skin and rig baked, mane and tail as skinned ribbons, textures baked to 2K, gaits resampled to 30 fps loops (idle, walk, trot, canter, gallop).
