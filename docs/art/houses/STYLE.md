# Town and village houses: concept art for Meshy image-to-3D

The ordinary houses of towns and villages, made by the square set's route ([square/STYLE.md](../square/STYLE.md),
[STANDARDS.md](../STANDARDS.md) §6) so a whole town reads as the Kingsmere square does. They replace the Quaternius
MegaKit houses (`realm/assets/village`). Concepts: `codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -i ref/<name>_ref.jpg --
"<square/style.txt> The asset: <line from pieces.tsv>"`, the square set's style prompt word for word. Crops from
[01-town](../target/01-town.jpg) in `ref/`. Red clay tile roofs take the house colour (masked like the square set);
thatch stays golden. Sizes per STANDARDS §2 (storey 3 m).
