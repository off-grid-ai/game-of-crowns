# Draft marketing copy

These drafts use the game's name and the role described in the repository. They do not state a release date, price, or store availability.

## Tagline options

- Keep the peace. Hold the crown.
- A living realm. A crown to keep.
- The realm moves. Your choices matter.

Suggested main line: **Keep the peace. Hold the crown.**

## One sentence

Rule Aldmere, a living medieval realm where great houses grow, scheme, and go to war.

## Short description

Game of Crowns puts you on the throne of Aldmere. Its great houses rule their lands and pursue their own aims. Use grain, gold, royal decrees, and the strength of the Crown to keep the peace while the realm prospers.

## Longer description

Aldmere is a living medieval realm. Its towns grow, its great houses seek power, and its people face the results of war, hunger, and royal decisions.

You are the King. Hear the court, support your vassals, judge disputes, and decide when to use the royal host. Each choice can help one house and place new pressure on another.

Game of Crowns follows a realm over time. Your task is to keep the peace while it prospers.
