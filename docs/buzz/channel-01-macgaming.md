**Title:** Free and native on Apple Silicon: a medieval kingdom sim where the lords remember what you did

I've been building this on my Mac and the first build is out today. Free, nothing locked.

Great houses run themselves: they farm, starve, marry and go to war without you. You're the King, and you can't move a single soldier. You rule with grain, gold, taxes, marriages and judgement at court. Refuse a lord's petition or call in a loan he can't pay, and he remembers.

It's a native Apple Silicon build, signed and notarised.
