# The virality factor (2026-11-05)

The game is playable and can go open source now. The question is why anyone would play it and tell someone else. This builds on angle.md (every lord remembers) and gtm.md (what spreads small games).

## The thesis
**Every 5-minute reign makes a story nobody else has, starring people you know.**

What spreads games right now is people sharing what the game did to them. Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream (2026) sold 8M+, and its feeds filled with players' friends and celebrities doing absurd things. Where Winds Meet and Suck Up! spread because players filmed what AI characters said. World sims spread through "look what happened" videos. We combine all three: your friends, as lords who remember, in a realm that runs itself.

## The four mechanics, in order
1. **Put your friends in the realm.** Name a lord, a lady or an heir after anyone. They scheme, marry, betray you and remember what you did to them. "My brother married into Thornvale and marched on my capital" is the screenshot. The game already keeps characters as plain files (DESIGN.md), so this is mostly a naming screen.
   - **Streamer mode:** chat names become the realm's lords. RimWorld, Kerbal Space Program and Despot's Game all have chat-as-characters for streamers; it gives viewers a reason to stay and a stake in the story.
2. **Every reign ends in a saga card.** One image: the King's epithet ("Mac the Unready"), the chain that defined the reign ("taxed Thornvale → Lord Edric remembered → the siege of 352"), and how it ended. Made to post, like Wordle's grid. Reigns players' favourite thing was its endings.
3. **The lords answer back.** "Rule in your own words" already exists. Players will type outrageous rulings to see what the lords say; that's the Where Winds Meet and Suck Up! clip. The simulation decides, the model gives the lord his voice, so it stays in character and never breaks the game.
4. **The daily realm.** Same seed for everyone each day, five minutes, compare your saga card with friends'.

## The spark: open source
- **GitHub + Show HN + r/LocalLLaMA:** "Game of Thrones x Westworld, open source: every lord runs on a local model, offline." That crowd tries things the day they see them, and Off Grid AI gets the credit.
- **r/godot:** the how-it's-built post.
- **Then creators:** cold-DM mid-size WorldBox, map-sim and strategy creators with streamer mode.

## How we'll know it's working
Not upvotes: saga cards and friend-realm screenshots posted by people we don't know, GitHub stars and downloads, and creators who stream it without being asked twice.
