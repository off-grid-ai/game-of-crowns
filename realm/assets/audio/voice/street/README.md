# The street's voices

The crier in the square (`crier_<n>`) and the stallholders crying their wares (`hawker_<n>`), heard near whoever calls
(`realm/data/sounds.json` `posts`: the crier's and a stallholder's Yelling). Kokoro `bm_daniel`, `am_adam` and
`am_michael`, near and dry, made with Off Grid AI Desktop's local Kokoro text-to-speech, shaped by ffmpeg and encoded
with oggenc; remake with `zsh realm/tools/record_calls.sh street`.
