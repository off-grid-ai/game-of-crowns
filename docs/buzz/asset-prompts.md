# Prompts for Codex: promo art

Style for all three (matches DESIGN.md's art direction): warm and painterly, never toy-like; muted colours, golden-hour light, soft haze; a medieval realm seen from a low oblique angle, never top-down; buildings small against a large landscape; gritty and grand, Game of Thrones in feel but no real show names, logos or likenesses; no dragons, no modern objects. Text, where asked, in a plain serif in warm off-white with a soft dark shadow.

## 1. GitHub social preview (1280 x 640, PNG under 1 MB)
> A wide, painterly key art image, 1280x640. A low oblique view across a medieval valley at golden hour: a walled stone town with a keep on a rise beside a river, timber-framed houses, fields in strips, a road winding out of frame. In the foreground, out of focus, a lord in a dark fur cloak with his back half-turned to us, looking at the town, one hand on a sword hilt: he is remembering something. Banners of two rival houses on distant towers. Warm haze, muted colours, painterly brushwork. Keep everything important inside the central 1200x600. Title text on the left third: "Game of Crowns" large, and under it smaller: "Every lord remembers." Nothing else written.

## 2. itch.io cover (630 x 500, PNG)
> Painterly medieval key art, 630x500, for a game store tile that is often shown small. A low oblique view of a hilltop castle town at golden hour with a river below and a single rider on the road toward it. Strong, simple silhouette that reads at thumbnail size. Title "Game of Crowns" across the top in a plain serif, warm off-white with a soft shadow. No other text.

## 3. Wide key art, no text (1920 x 1080, PNG)
> The same world as the images above, 1920x1080, no text. A great hall at dusk seen from the side: a lord kneeling before an empty throne with a petition, courtiers watching, a long table with a map of the realm, candle and window light, painterly and gritty. The lord's face is hard, like someone who hasn't forgotten an old wrong. For trailers, posts and a future Steam page.

After making them: put 1 in GitHub (repo Settings > Social preview; it can't be set from the command line), 2 on the itch.io page, and drop all three in `docs/buzz/images/` so the supervisor can use them.
