# Codex brief: the launch film, "The Rose Remembers" (6 paintings)

Paste everything below the line into Codex.

---

Paint 6 still images for a 30-second launch film for **Game of Crowns**, a medieval kingdom strategy game set in the realm of **Aldmere**. They tell one short story, so they must read as one set and share the same characters. Each image fills the screen for 3–5 seconds with a slow push-in, and the same image is also cropped to a vertical 9:16 phone version.

## The story the images tell
Ten years ago, in a war between two great houses, the King sided against House Thornvale (green banners, a rose). Thornvale lost the field, and Lord Varn of Thornvale died there. His son **Gareth Varn** was 14. Now Gareth is 24, and he stands in the King's hall and says: "Ten years ago, you took our enemy's side in war. House Thornvale has not forgotten it." The player decides what to say back.

## Rules for every image (the same as the intro set)
- **Style:** match `realm/assets/ui/title-background.png` and the intro paintings in `realm/assets/intro/art/` (look at `hall.jpg`, `battle.jpg`, `hero.jpg`, `house_thornvale.jpg` first). Use the same warm ochre and amber light, moss greens, blue-grey distance, muted crimson, soft luminous cloud and fine layered painterly brushwork. Grounded, hand-painted historical concept art: not photoreal, not cartoon. 13th–14th century dress and arms only. No fantasy creatures, magic or modern objects.
- **Size:** 3840×2160 (16:9) PNG. If you can't make 4K, make 1920×1080 and say so.
- **Two crops from one image:** keep the subject (face, figure, the key action) inside the **centre 40% of the width**, so a vertical 9:16 crop from the middle still holds the whole story beat. Keep the outer sides calm and atmospheric, but filled.
- **Room for words:** keep the bottom third quiet and a little darker (floor, ground, shadow, water). Put nothing important there. Don't add a caption panel or gradient graphic.
- **Camera:** low and human-height, oblique, never top-down.
- **No lettering anywhere:** no text, numbers, signatures, logos or watermarks.
- **Heraldry:** Thornvale is deep green cloth (#4f8a3c) with at most a plain rose silhouette. The enemy house is grey cloth (#9aa6b2) with at most a plain wolf silhouette. The Crown is red cloth (#b8202a) with at most a plain gold crown silhouette. If a sigil comes out garbled, leave the banner plain.
- **Gareth Varn**, the same person in every image he's in: dark hair worn long to the jaw, pale grey eyes, a strong straight brow, lean. At 14: a thin boy in a green wool cloak. At 24: a young lord in a dark green surcoat over mail, a plain rose brooch at the throat, and no crown or ornate armour. He's grave, not snarling. Don't make him the likeness of any known actor.

Save the images to `~/Downloads/Aldmere/launch/` with these exact names, and report each file's name and size.

## The six images

1. **`launch_gareth_hall.png`**, the cold open: Gareth Varn at 24, standing alone in the aisle of the King's great hall (the hall in `hall.jpg`, seen this time from just behind and beside the throne, low). He faces the camera and the empty throne, mid-speech, chin up. His eyes are on the King, who is off-screen. Torchlight is warm on one side of his face and the other side is in shadow. Courtiers are blurred and small behind him, and a green Thornvale banner hangs faint at the back. Mood: a debt is being called in. His face sits at about x 50%, y 32%.

2. **`launch_war_315.png`**, the King's call ten years earlier: a battle in Thornvale's green valley under a grey storm sky with a break of light. The camera stands on the losing side, low, behind the green Thornvale line. Ahead, grey-wolf banners advance, and riding in on their flank, decisive, come the Crown's red banners. The King has picked a side. Smoke rises from an orchard. No gore. The red banners arriving sit at about x 50%, y 35%.

3. **`launch_boy_orchard.png`**, why he remembers: after the battle. The 14-year-old Gareth kneels in a burnt orchard at dusk beside a fallen green rose banner, holding his father's sword across his knees. His father's body isn't shown. Behind him, red Crown banners and grey wolf banners ride away on the far road, small. He watches them go. Ash drifts and there's a last ember glow. The boy sits at about x 50%, y 40%, side-lit.

4. **`launch_years.png`**, ten years pass: the same orchard, regrown and in blossom, morning light. Grown Gareth, now 24, trains alone with that same sword beside the one blackened stump that never grew back. Mood: patience, not peace. Gareth and the stump sit within x 35–65%.

5. **`launch_gathering.png`**, what comes next: dusk on a ridge. Gareth Varn sits on horseback, seen from behind and slightly to the side, looking down a long valley at the distant lights of the King's city on its lake (Kingsmere, as in `house_crown.jpg`, far away). Behind him on the ridge, a line of Thornvale riders with green banners and a few torches is just lit. Not yet an attack, but a threat. Gareth sits at about x 50%, y 38%.

6. **`launch_kingsmere_dusk.png`**, the end-card ground: Kingsmere, the royal castle on its lake, at blue hour from the far shore. The castle is lit and calm, the water still, the sky deep. Keep the centre band, the upper middle third, open and dark enough for a title to sit over it, with the castle low on the horizon at about y 55–65%. Leave the bottom third as quiet dark water.
