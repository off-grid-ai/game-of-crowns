# levy: the levied spearmen

The pikemen's own body (meshy_town_guard: the Meshy body with empty hands, no shield, a kettle hat) with the same
Meshy pike prop scaled to 0.45, about a 2 m spear (Mac, 2026-11-13: no new Meshy generation; make the levy from what
we have). Rigged and VAT-baked locally by realm/tools/rig/rig_unit.py (rig.json units.levy, the pikeman_body row with
the prop's scale): same clips as the pikemen, the jab at the attack. Plain undyed cloth with the house colour only at
the shoulders is set in data (army.json units.levy.cloth). Sources: ~/code/goc-kits/meshy/army/pikeman_body/,
~/code/goc-kits/meshy/army/pike/pike_prop.glb.
