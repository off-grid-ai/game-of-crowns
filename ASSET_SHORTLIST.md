# Aldmere asset shortlist: realistic medieval, free

Research only. Nothing was downloaded into the project.

**How this was checked**
- Sketchfab: API metadata (likes, faces, licence, animation count) plus a look at each model's thumbnail for the picks marked "seen".
- Poly Haven: IDs taken straight from `api.polyhaven.com`. Direct URL pattern is `https://polyhaven.com/a/<ID>`.
- Fab: the site can't be queried without a browser, so Fab is not itemised. See the Fab note under Gaps.
- Sketchfab links use the model's UID. All downloads there need your Sketchfab login (choose glTF, or "Autoconverted format (glTF)"). Poly Haven and ambientCG need no login.
- Licence: CC-BY unless stated. Credit the author if you ever ship.
- Face counts are as listed on Sketchfab. Over ~150k is flagged HEAVY.

## The people problem (be honest)

Free, realistic, rigged medieval peasants and soldiers **at game-ready poly counts basically don't exist in quantity**. What is realistic to get:

1. **The Fuse Civilians and Woman (A5).** Mixamo-rigged, about 21–25k faces, realistic proportions, with 3 male and 1 female. They are the only free set I'd call "Manor Lords-adjacent". They give you a small cast with obvious repeats, so vary them with texture tints and scale.
2. **Mixamo (free, needs an Adobe login).** Upload any T-pose humanoid, get an auto-rig and a library of walk, run, idle, work and sword animations. This is the answer to "riggable so animations can be applied". It's also where the animations for items 16–19 and Pack B come from. Mixamo has no good medieval characters of its own.
3. **MPFB for Blender (free, CC0 output) or MakeHuman.** Builds realistic men and women of any age and build, with a standard rig. Combine them with clothing such as Red_Ilya's outfit. This is the only way to get real variety, but it is manual work in Blender. I'm flagging it as an option, not a download.
4. **Armour for soldiers** comes from the unrigged Kelisei Light Armor and the Condottiere, via Mixamo.

**Not available free:** a full crowd of varied, rigged, period-correct peasants, women, children, animals, and soldiers carrying different weapons. Expect 4–6 distinct bodies at best.

## Gaps: what can't be found free at this quality

- **Church or chapel.** I found only cathedrals and ruins at 0.5M–2.5M faces, which are too heavy. No realistic, light village church turned up. This is the biggest building gap. Options are a decimated heavy scan, or Fab (below).
- **Smithy.** No standalone realistic smithy building. Use a house from Pack A plus the Tools Set and Grindstone as props, with Poly Haven `stone_fire_pit`.
- **Gatehouse as its own piece.** The Medieval Castle in A1 contains one; a standalone gatehouse is not available free.
- **Temperate broadleaf trees (oak, beech, birch).** Poly Haven has only conifers and one small broadleaf. This is the biggest landscape gap. Check Sketchfab for "oak tree" and "birch tree" (not searched), or the Fab free Megaplants/Megascans below.
- **Consistent style.** Everything here is from different authors. It sits in the "realistic PBR" band but isn't one art direction. Expect to unify with a shared colour grade, fog, and a common roof/wall texture swap.
- **Fab.** Not itemised, because the site needs a browser session and I wasn't able to check it. Web sources conflict on current terms. Quixel Megascans were free on Fab only until end of 2024 (assets claimed then stay usable under the Fab Standard licence, including in other engines). The legacy Bridge library is Unreal-only. Megaplants are reported as free. **Action for you:** log into Fab and search "medieval" and "megascans" with the Free filter, and check each listing's licence for non-Unreal use before relying on it.
- **Animals, farm life, crops, children.** Not covered at all.
- **Performance.** At Manor Lords scale, even these 10k–25k meshes need LODs and, for houses, ideally simplified distant versions. That's a Godot-side job.
