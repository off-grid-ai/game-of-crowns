# Public demand: how Game of Crowns should feel

Research, 10 Nov 2026. Public data only. **[est]** marks an estimate (a third-party model, a rough count, or our reading).

## The answer (one screen)

**Demand points to a hybrid: Crusader Kings' people, inside Manor Lords' living town, decided through Reigns' faces.**
Frostpunk adds the pressure: a season or threat that forces the hard calls. It is not the core.

- **The stage is the living town.** Players flock to it. The engine is people with agendas. It is what keeps them playing.
- **Reigns is the way you decide, not the game.** Fast, face-to-face choices are loved, but on their own they turn into "repetitive".
- **The gap nobody fills:** CK3 players say the world feels dead and is just map painting. Manor Lords players say there's nothing to do and no lords or diplomacy. A town you can see, run by schemers you have to rule, is the open seat.

**Strongest evidence**

1. **The living town pulls the biggest crowd.** Manor Lords sold 1M copies in its first day and peaked at **173,178 concurrent players**, the record for any Steam city builder (beat Civ 6's 162,657). It is reported at **4M sales** (Aug 2026). ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/city-builder/manor-lords-shatters-steam-city-builder-record-with-over-170000-concurrent-players), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/city-builder/manor-lords-breaks-a-steam-city-builder-and-strategy-game-record-as-it-sells-1-million-copies-in-just-one-day-after-release), [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/2026/08/manor-lords-celebrates-4-million-sales-with-a-huge-new-update/)) But players drop off once they've seen it all: "after 5-10 rounds there's nothing more to do", and they are "waiting for more lords… and some diplomacy". ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/city-builder/hooded-horse-ceo-hits-back-at-claims-that-manor-lords-failed-in-early-access-not-every-game-should-be-aimed-at-becoming-some-live-service-boom-or-bust), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1363080/discussions/0/4358998116398944407))
2. **People with agendas keep players for years.** CK3 has sold **4M+** copies (Apr 2025) and holds **90% positive across 142,903 reviews**. Its top praise is "replayable, emergent stories". ([Paradox](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/media/press-releases/paradox-interactive/crusader-kings-iii-passes-four-million-sales), [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1158310/Crusader_Kings_III), [VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/1158310/crusader_kings_iii))
3. **Game of Thrones demand is real, and fans are meeting it with a mod.** CK3's *A Game of Thrones* mod has **667,275 subscribers**, roughly 1 in 6 CK3 owners **[est]**. Fans asked of the official GoT RTS: "a dedicated Game of Thrones strategy game that I don't have to look for in mods?" ([Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2962333032), [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/game-of-thrones-strategiespiel-einige-spieler-wollen-anderes-genre,1582459/))
4. **The exact hybrid is already being asked for.** *Norland*, pitched as a medieval town builder with "Game of Thrones-style backstabbing" (RimWorld × CK3), hit **500K+ wishlists** before launch. It sits at **82% positive across 10,199 reviews**. ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/city-builder/manor-lords-publisher-celebrates-500000-wishlists-for-another-medieval-city-building-strategy-game-with-more-sex-drama-and-game-of-thrones-style-backstabbing), [Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1857090/Norland))
5. **Fast choices alone wear thin, and so do numbers without faces.** In Reigns' reviews, "repetitive and boring" (97 mentions) beats "fun" (65). Reigns: Game of Thrones holds only **~77% of ~930 reviews**. Frostpunk 2 moved from people to districts, and "sequel falls short" is its top complaint (28). Critics called it "lost in a sea of numbers". ([VaporLens Reigns](https://vaporlens.app/app/474750/reigns), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/897820), [VaporLens FP2](https://vaporlens.app/app/1601580/frostpunk_2), [JumpDashRoll](https://www.jumpdashroll.com/article/frostpunk-2-review))

**The 3 things players most want from a king or Game of Thrones game**

1. **Houses and people who scheme on their own.** Betrayal, marriages and rivals that make their own stories, not scripted events. (CK3's top praise. Its top gameplay complaint is "random events instead of emergent situations".)
2. **A world you can see living and changing.** Towns, people and land that answer what you do. (Manor Lords' visuals: 30 mentions. CK3 asks: "make the world more… alive".)
3. **Choices with real costs you see play out.** Not illusory or luck-based choices. (Frostpunk: moral choices 52 and atmosphere 96. Reigns: "illusory choices" 16 and "luck-based" 30 as complaints.)

**What this means for us [est]:** every decision a face brings you lands visibly on the map within moments. The houses act and plot between your decisions. That closes exactly the gap players name in all four games.

## Method and limits

- **Reddit is blocked for our tools** (WebSearch returns 400 for reddit.com). Steam reviews, Steam discussions, press coverage and VaporLens stood in for it. VaporLens tallies themes from a sample of Steam reviews of unstated size, so every theme count below is **[est]** and only good for ranking themes within one game.
- SteamDB returns 403 to our fetcher. CCU peaks come from SteamDB figures quoted in the press. Raijin's "all-time peak" covers only its own tracking window, so we use it for current players only.
- Steam doesn't publish sales. Any "copies" from Raijin, VaporLens or SteamSpy is a model **[est]**. Publisher figures are marked "official".

**What the numbers say [est]:**
- **Biggest launch pull:** living town (Manor Lords 173K) and lord-in-a-sandbox (Bannerlord 248K).
- **Longest hold:** agendas. CK3 still has 15K concurrent six years in, ~4× Manor Lords and Frostpunk today.
- **Pressure** sells steadily (Frostpunk 5M, Against the Storm 2M). The sequel that dropped the human scale lost the crowd (FP2: 75% positive, ~446 players).
- **Faces-and-choices** sells wide on mobile but holds few PC players. Reigns' GoT licence scored *below* the original (77% vs 87%).

## 2. What players praise and ask for

Theme counts from VaporLens review samples **[est]**. Bracketed figures are mentions.

**Crusader Kings III**: praise: dynasty sim (22), roleplay inside grand strategy (17), replayable, emergent stories (17), warfare and empire (16), mods (15). Complaints: DLC price and paywalls (23+17), performance (19). Steam discussions on boredom: "becomes a map painter", "clicking the same RNG events over and over", "random events popping up instead of emergent situations", the AI is "so passive", Paradox could "make the world more interesting and alive". ([Steam search set](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1158310/discussions/0/4353368163567181439))

**Manor Lords**: praise: city-building (51), economy depth (36), beauty (30), medieval setting (30). Complaints: unfinished (24), slow Early Access (22+18), crashes (14). The design complaint is content: "nothing more to do after 5-10 rounds", "a 'nothing new' plateau" once the town is big. Fans want "more lords, who build too, and some diplomacy". Steam users call it a possible "city building CK3". ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1363080/discussions/0/594013174543495450))

**Frostpunk**: praise: immersive atmosphere (96), "survival governance" (83), challenge (55), emotional storytelling (23). Complaints: difficulty (81), repetitive (77), emotional toll of moral dilemmas (75). Mixed: moral choices and consequences (52). Protests, strikes and demanding citizens (34) are part of what players remember.

**Frostpunk 2**: praise: factions and politics (39), Utopia Builder (33), districts (27). Complaints: falls short of the original (28), too different (22), overwhelming (15), UI (14). Critics: "lacks the small scale and… human narrative of its predecessor".

**Reigns**: praise: fun (65), narrative (25), multiple endings (23), decisions (21). Complaints: **repetitive (97)**, confusing (41), luck-based (30), illusory choices (16). Critics loved the idea. The Guardian said binary choices limited depth.

**Bannerlord**: praise: battles (32), mods (26), freedom (21), rich world (12), kingdom and clan management (11). Complaints: repetitive (21), **"basic diplomacy" (18)**, AI behaviour (13).

## 3. Gaps in the market

| Game | What players say is missing | Where the demand goes |
|---|---|---|
| CK3 | A world that feels alive. Events feel random, not emergent. Late game is map painting | Seeing the realm itself: towns, people, the land |
| Manor Lords | Politics, rival lords who act, diplomacy, a late game | People with agendas around the town |
| Frostpunk 2 | The human scale: faces, individual stories | Faces and named people under pressure |
| Reigns | Depth and lasting consequence. Choices feel illusory or luck-based | A world that remembers and visibly changes |
| Bannerlord | Diplomacy and politics beyond "basic" | A court and houses that matter |
| Official GoT games | A strategy or RPG on houses and intrigue (fans settle for a CK3 mod) | A GoT-like kingship game |

**The open seat [est]:** each game's complaint is another game's strength. CK3 has the people but no visible world. Manor Lords has the world but no people. Reigns has the faces but no memory. One game where scheming houses bring their quarrels to your face, and the answer shows up in a town you can watch, fills all three gaps. Norland's 500K wishlists show the market is waiting for it. Its 82% shows nobody has nailed it yet.

## 4. Steam tags and trends

- **City Builder × Medieval is the hottest launch tag pair.** Manor Lords holds the all-time City Builder CCU record (173K > Civ 6 162K > Cities: Skylines 2 105K). Hooded Horse's medieval slate (Manor Lords, Against the Storm, Norland) is the publisher signal. ([TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/102790-manor-lords-achieves-steam-most-successful-city-builder.html))
- **Political Sim is a small tag with high scores.** SteamSpy lists 14 games, average user score 83%, ~16M owners in total. CK2 leads (2–5M), then Tropico and Victoria. ([SteamSpy](https://steamspy.com/tag/Political+Sim), data marked stale since mid-2025) Few games carry the tag, and they rate well. A supply gap **[est]**.
- **Medieval + Political Sim + Choices Matter + City Builder** combined: only small titles (Medieval Legacy, 63 reviews; Feudal Baron). Nothing big holds this combination **[est]**. ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/medieval-legacy/info))
- **Wishlist signals:** Norland crossed 500K pre-launch (top ~70 most-wishlisted on Steam at 350K). War for Westeros, the official GoT strategy game, slipped to early 2027. It is an RTS, which leaves the court-and-houses lane open. ([CGM](https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/game-of-thrones-war-for-westeros-delay/))
- **Trend [est]:** tension-builders with faces hold (Frostpunk 93%, Against the Storm 2M). Scale-up sequels that lose the faces slip (FP2). Fast choice games sell to mobile, not PC.
