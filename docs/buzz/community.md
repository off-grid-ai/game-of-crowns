# Building our own community, from gamers (2026-11-05)

Mac's call: not r/LocalLLaMA, Hacker News or the local-AI crowd (exhausted, allergic). We build a new community and win the gaming crowd. Builds on angle.md, virality.md and acquisition.md.

## The home: a Discord called the court
Discord keeps people; it doesn't find them (gtm.md). So the reason to join has to be the game itself:

**Join and you're written into the realm.** Every member becomes a lord, lady or knight in the Founders' Realm, a realm that keeps running. Your lord remembers what the King did to him, schemes, marries and may rise.

**The weekly reign.** Once a week the court plays a season or two of the Founders' Realm together: members vote on the petitions, the realm answers, and the saga card goes up. Members see what their own lord did and share it ("my lord just married into Thornvale and turned on the King").

This is virality.md's "friends in the realm" turned into a community ritual. It needs the naming feature (already asked of the main session); the weekly reign can be run by hand at first.

## Where the first members come from (gaming only)
1. **r/macgaming** (~209k members). The game is a free, native Apple Silicon game, and Mac gamers have few of those. This fits today's Mac-only build exactly.
   - Title: *Free and native on Apple Silicon: a kingdom sim where every lord remembers what you did*
2. **Mac gaming creators:** Andrew Tsai (~109k subscribers, covers native Mac games) first, then smaller ones.
3. **r/SimulationGames and r/4Xgaming:** posts.md, led by a clip of a lord remembering.
4. **Comments, not posts,** in WorldBox, Ages of Conflict, map-sim and r/CK3AGOT "games like this" threads.
5. **r/godot:** the how-it's-built post. It's a game-dev community, not the local-AI one.

Every post and reply ends with the same invite: *join the court and you'll be written into the realm.*

**Wave 2, after a Windows build:** gaming creators (30 cold DMs with streamer mode), r/indiegames, a free Steam page, short clips handed to creators.

## No single shot
Nothing here depends on one post landing: no Show HN, no "launch day". Each channel above is repeatable, and the weekly reign gives a new saga card, clip and reason to post every week. A post that flops costs one try; the routine keeps going.

## How we talk to gamers
Lead with the game: Game of Thrones politics, lords who remember, a reign in minutes, free. Never lead with "AI" or "local model"; answer plainly if asked.

## What to measure
Discord joins per channel (one invite link per channel), members who come back for a second weekly reign, saga cards posted by members.
