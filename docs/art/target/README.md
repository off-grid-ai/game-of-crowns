# The art target (Mac approved, 2026-11-11: "this is the level that we wanted to be at")

Eight concept images, made with the Codex CLI from the prompts in this folder, are the bar for the look and for
how the world reacts. Every visual change is judged beside the matching image at the same framing.

| Image | What it sets |
|---|---|
| 01-town | The capital at roof height: dense timber/thatch/tile plots, a stone castle in house colours, people visibly at work, fields beyond |
| 02-realm | The realm map: house colours with crisp borders, contested land striped, castles, roads, armies as coloured columns (our own heraldry, not the image's) |
| 03-army | A real army: dense formed blocks (spears and shields, swords, axes, archers, knights, elephants, cannons and catapults) in one house's colours |
| 04-siege | A wall cracking into a breach, attackers pouring through, real shaped flames and smoke on burning houses |
| 05-court | A ruling: a painted portrait card, and the result playing out in the town |
| 06-inn | Life on the great road: an inn, horses, soldiers at tables, a merchant, a shrine and a milestone |
| 07-unrest | A displeased house: torn, lowered banners, an angry crowd, doubled guards, shuttered shops |
| 08-reinforce | The royal host marching to reinforce an ally and garrison its town |

Fidelity: polished mid (Mac's picker likes: Foundation, AoE2 DE, AoM Retold, Settlers 7, SpellForce 3, Warcraft III).
The style prompt is style-prompt.txt; the scenes are scene-prompts.txt.

**House colours everywhere (Mac, 2026-11-11: "that is good, that is smart, that is what we should do… this is your
North Star"):** buildings and uniforms wear the holding house's colours, as in 02-realm. Roof tiles, trims, shutters,
awnings and banners take the colour; walls and stone stay natural. A town that changes hands recolours. One shared
team-colour mask shader serves buildings and armour (the Warcraft III approach).
