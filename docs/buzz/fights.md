# What players complain about, and which fights we take (2026-11-05)

Sources: Steam review themes from vaporlens.app (counts are review mentions), SteamSpy, and the search notes in demand.md.

## The complaints

| Complaint | Where (mentions) | Can we win it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Boring after a few hours, passive, no goals** | WorldBox, Ages of Conflict, Fantasy Map Sim ("passive, boring" 26), Manor Lords ("monotonous" late game) | **Yes.** The realm brings trouble to you: petitions, wars, famines, a pretender. Something is always at stake. |
| **Steep learning curve, needs a wiki** | CK3 (19), RimWorld (20), Norland (weak tutorial), Fantasy Map Sim (wants a tutorial) | **Yes, with the AI.** An adviser who knows your realm and tells you what matters and what each choice costs. That's the on-device companion. |
| **Repetitive events and text** | CK3 (fixed with mods), Whispers from the Star ("repetitive, shallow" conversations) | **Yes, if we keep the model on a short leash.** Lines come from what actually happened in your realm, so they differ every reign. Open-ended chat on a small model is where AI games fail. |
| **DLC prices** | CK3 (93, the top complaint), RimWorld ($100+ of DLC, 17) | **Yes, by policy.** One price, no DLC, no in-app purchases. |
| **Paying for AI tokens, always online, privacy** | AI2U (tokens run out), Whispers from the Star (disconnections 22, privacy 5), cloud NPCs with 3–4 second pauses | **Yes, outright.** On-device: no tokens, no subscription, works offline, nothing leaves your machine. No cloud AI game can match this. |
| **Bugs, crashes, performance** | Top 3 for Manor Lords, inZOI, Norland, CK3 | **Must not lose.** A small, stable game beats a big buggy one. Not a selling point; a floor. |
| **Graphics** | Manor Lords' top love is its visuals (30); inZOI "stunning graphics" | **No.** Good-looking is enough. Never lead with screenshots of fidelity against Manor Lords. |
| **"AI" as a label** | Steam reviews mark down games that disclose generative AI; most gamers are negative on it | **Not head-on.** Sell what the player gets ("an adviser who knows your realm", "they remember you"). Be open about the local model when asked; in r/LocalLLaMA, lead with it. |

## The USP (Mac, 2026-11-05)
**A whole reign in 5 minutes.** Years turn fast, a lot happens, and you only make the big calls, so it fits in the time you have. A 5-minute reign is a real setting today (a year is about 4 minutes at normal pace; reigns can be timed at 5, 10 or 60 minutes, or endless).

Short-session strategy sells: Reigns (a king making quick calls) 500k–1M owners on Steam alone; Against the Storm, Bad North, Dorfromantik 0.5–2M each (SteamSpy). It also answers the deep games' complaints head-on: CK3's learning curve and 20–30 minute loads, WorldBox getting boring after a few hours (fine when a session is five minutes).

## The fights we pick
0. **Five minutes is enough.** The lead.
1. **Never boring.** A world that runs itself and keeps putting real choices in front of you.
2. **Depth without the manual.** The game's adviser knows your realm and explains it. On your machine.
3. **Pay once. No DLC, no tokens, no internet needed.**

Fight 2 is the one only we can make: a cloud AI game can't run free and offline, and a big studio's strategy game doesn't ship an adviser who reads the live state of your realm.
