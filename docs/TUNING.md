# Tuning while you play

Each line: to change X, edit `<file>`: `<key>`. Bridge files take effect after the bridge restarts
(`launchctl kickstart -k gui/$(id -u)/com.realm.bridge`); view files at the game's next launch.
Switches (`--name`) go after `--` on the game's command line.

## Camera (Builder 2)
- How close the camera may come (the roof-height floor): `realm/data/camera.json`: `floor`.
- How far a town, a castle or a followed host is framed from: `realm/data/camera.json`: `go_to.*`.
- How many headings "go to" tries before settling for the least blocked: `realm/data/camera.json`: `sight_steps`.
- How close a roof, wall or tree may come before it dissolves out of the view (gone nearer than, whole from): `realm/data/camera.json`: `near_fade.buildings`, `near_fade.trees`, `near_fade.castle` (a castle's walls, sooner).
- How a still or film frames a town's square (`--city=`): from the side away from its castle, turned a little, the square set right of the town card: `realm/data/camera.json`: `town_shot.*`.
- How a siege is framed in the films (the breach step; 04-siege): its distance, how far it looks down, how far the heading turns off straight out from the town so the wall runs on a diagonal, and how far toward the camp the aim sits: `realm/data/camera.json`: `siege_view.*`.
- Where the flat atlas comes in over the 3D land (only just before the painted overview takes over at distance 60; below it the 3D land is all there is): `realm/data/camera.json`: `map.atlas_from`, `map.atlas_to`, `map.atlas_most`.
- How far from the lens a town's houses, halls and castle still draw (so a town stays a town at mid zoom; Godot's detail levels lighten them): `realm/data/camera.json`: `map.reach.*` (clutter, small, house, great, castle).

## Castles (Builder 2)
- Where a seat's castle may stand when the first search fails (headings, rings, sizes, water allowed at its edge): `realm/data/towns.json`: `castle.*`.

## The director: threats and the shape of a reign (Builder 2)
- How quiet the realm must be, and for how long, before a threat rises: `bridge/data/director.json`: `quietBelow`, `quietSeasons`, `quietByAct` (by act).
- The breather after any crisis, in seasons: `bridge/data/director.json`: `breather`.
- When the last act's finale lands, as a share of the reign's clock: `bridge/data/director.json`: `finale.landAt`.
- Each threat's growth, words and the orders that answer it: `bridge/data/director.json`: `threats.<kind>.*`.
- How a too-comfortable King draws envy: `bridge/data/director.json`: `pushback.*`.
- Risings (a great house split by Freeciv's civil war; `bridge/risings.js`): the seasons of warning from a claim to the roll, how many seasons a house sits cold before a claimant stands up, the smallest house that can split, and what each answer at court moves: `bridge/data/risings.json`. The rebels' host (companies and kinds), its march (tiles a season) and the seasons before a rising settles itself: `bridge/data/realmrules/luadata.txt` `[risings]`. The odds are Freeciv's own: `Civil_War_Chance` per government in `bridge/data/realmrules/effects.ruleset`, ±5 a restless or feasting town (`game.ruleset` `civil_war_bonus_*`). Probe: `bridge/tools/rising_probe.mjs`.
- Raids (men without banners, paid or in a story) are Freeciv's barbarians raised beside the town (`realm_raid` in `bridge/data/realmrules/script.lua`). From which turn they can come: `bridge/data/realmrules/game.ruleset` `onsetbarbs` (1). What each raiders choice means in Freeciv (None, Some = huts only, Hordes): `BARBARIANS` in `bridge/chronicles.js`. How many seasons a raid lasts before the raiders leave (they pillage the land by the town each season, Freeciv's Pillage order): `bridge/data/realmrules/luadata.txt` `[raids]` `seasons` (8). When it is over they walk off to wild land or the map's edge and go there: `leave_steps` (3 tiles a season), `leave_reach` (30 steps looked for a way out), `leave_seasons` (4; then any still walking go where they stand, as do any with no way out). How strong a raid comes (Wealth draws the raid: beside Freeciv's own few riders, a band sized by the town): `band` (3) men plus one for every `band_per` (3) of the town's size, of `kinds` in turn (Legion, Archers, Horsemen), at `veteran` level (2, hardened). Raiders never take a town (they kill men and pillage): the `PlayerState Barbarian` line in both conquer enablers of `bridge/data/realmrules/actions.ruleset`. Probe: `bridge/tools/raid_probe.mjs`.

## The King's season: royal seals (Builder 2)
- On or off for reigns that don't say: `bridge/data/seals.json`: `on`. A new reign can set it with `options.seals`.
- Seals a season, and how many he can keep: `bridge/data/seals.json`: `perSeason`, `bank`, `bankLastAct`.
- How near a threat must be to top the "next thing" line: `bridge/data/next.json`: `soon`.
- The uses always on offer for a royal seal, after the urgent ones (a feast or a ward for the coldest house, the banners, walls, the levy): `bridge/data/next.json`: `standing`.

## Decrees and their prices (Builder 2)
- Each decree's price and words: `bridge/data/decrees.json`: `<decree>.cost`, `.label`.

## The court (Builder 2)
- How often the court sits and how many it hears, by stage of the reign: `bridge/data/court.json`: `stages`, `noClock`.
- What the reign's petitions setting (rare, often) shifts: `bridge/data/court.json`: `petitions`, `wantMin`, `wantMax`.
- How often the Hand may call the King in for a crisis between sittings: `bridge/data/court.json`: `handYear`.
- How many seasons until a lord comes back about a weighty ruling: `bridge/data/court.json` `rulingDue`.
- The council's two voices on every matter (the Hand urges his own house's answer under a pretext, the Steward the cheapest or warmest other one): titles, pretexts and the Steward's lines, `bridge/data/court.json` `voices`; the next-thing line's two voices back on, `bridge/data/next.json` `oneVoice` (false).

## Event cards (Builder 2)
- On or off for reigns that don't say: `bridge/data/events.json`: `settings.on` (a reign can set `options.events`).
- How likely a card is each season, and in a breather: `bridge/data/events.json`: `settings.chance`, `settings.chanceInBreather`.
- How long before a card can come again: `bridge/data/events.json`: `settings.rest` (or a card's own `rest`).
- Which decks a breather may draw, and how much war and famine weigh in the build-up: `bridge/data/events.json`: `settings.breatherDecks`, `settings.boost`.
- A card's words, choices, costs and what doing nothing costs: `bridge/data/events.json`: `cards[]`.
- How long the King has to answer a card (one matter at a time, brought by a rider from its town; it lapses after): `bridge/data/events.json`: `settings.deadline` (2 seasons from the rider's arrival, or a card's own `deadline`); how fast the rider rides to the King's seat: `settings.rider.speed` (12 tiles a season; the card can't be answered until he arrives).

## The realm's clock and the houses' favour (NPC perf; changeability audit)
- How long a season lasts, in every realm: `bridge/data/pace.json`: `season`. The view reads it from /world (`turnSeconds`); crops, snow, sieges and the recruits' drill follow it.
- How fast or slow the speed buttons may set a season: `bridge/data/pace.json`: `fastest`, `slowest`.
- What a house's favour means (loyal, sworn, steady, cooling): `bridge/data/favour.json`. The houses panel's words, the court, counsel, the reign's goal and the townsfolk's mood all follow it.
- How long a cold house sits before its lord sends the King an ultimatum, and from a refused ultimatum to its host marching: `bridge/data/favour.json` `turning` (`seasons`, `riseIn`).
- When the treasury counts as thin (the Hand says so, the court may hear of it): `bridge/data/economy.json`: `thin`.
- How many seasons the King's own works take to rise: `bridge/data/economy.json` `workSeasons`.
- How much the King's deeds must add up to on an axis before the realm names him by it: `bridge/data/ledger.json` `strong`.
- The realm's stories: how many run at once, a season's chance one begins, how many one person carries, how long a scheme stays on the map after its last news, how long a town stays sick after Freeciv's plague: `bridge/data/stories.json` `maxActive`, `startChance`, `maxEach`, `schemeSeasons`, `plagueSeasons`.
- How many seasons a rumour keeps travelling: `bridge/data/thoughts.json` `rumourSeasons`. Probe for all of these: `bridge/tools/tunables_probe.mjs`.

## Measuring (NPC perf)
- A frame-time run drawn as the game is played, not at film quality: `--perf` with `--shot`'s framing (`--perf=smooth` or `--perf=beautiful` to pick).

## The world's models (NPC perf; the asset catalogue)
- Which model draws each world component (houses, tavern, hall, barn, mill, castle, church, carts, stalls, fences, clutter, ruins), its true size, the parts the land supplies itself, how often each variant is picked: `realm/data/assets.json`: `components.<id>.variants`. Forests, crops, people and the built keeps and towers join as their placers move over.

## Worn paths (NPC perf; finesse catalogue D3)
- Paths worn where people walk, growing back where they don't: `realm/data/ground.json`: `wear.on` (on); how fast it wears and grows back, how often it's stamped and drawn, the bare earth's colour: `wear.*`.

## Sizes (NPC perf)
- How tall each world component should stand, in metres: `realm/data/assets.json`: `components.<id>.heightRange`; `realm/tools/scale_check.gd` fails any model outside it. A model whose length misleads is scaled by its true height instead: a variant's `height`.

## The house colours and the target town (art target 01-town, 02-realm)
- The political map's wash by zoom: `realm/data/look.json`: `politics.wash_near`, `wash_far`, and `map_from` / `map_to` (the camera's height over the ground where the wash, band and stripes come in; below it the land stays natural and only the border line shows); `politics.on` (on).
- The realm at map zoom: a house's land edged, not filled: `realm/data/look.json` `politics.wash_far` (the tint inside, now 0.25), `politics.band` and `band_strength` (its colour strong just inside the border: how deep, a share of a tile, and how strong), `border_px` (the line); the land's hills shaded at map zoom: `realm/data/map.json` `relief.strength` (0 none) and `relief.light`.
- The same `map_from` / `map_to` hold back the plain (politics off) far wash of a house's colour, so the land round a town stays painted ground at mid zoom.
- How soon the sea's swell settles into a flat sheen away from the lens (further out it aliased into stripes): `realm/data/look.json`: `sea.waves_fade`.
- The sea's colours (deep, shallows, sheen; target 02-realm's blue-teal): `realm/data/look.json`: `sea.colours` (the water shader's own parameter names).
- Where a house's colour sits on its buildings: `realm/data/look.json`: `team.materials` (roof tiles, shutters `MI_Shutter`, timber `MI_WoodTrim`, banners), `team.ranks` (which components are ordinary houses), `team.paint.ordinary_share` (an ordinary house's share of the colour on its trims and shutters), `team.paint.saturation`, `team.paint.warm` (the colour toned to painted reds); `team.on` (on).
- Each house's roof (thatch, terracotta, dark tile or the house's colour), for cottages and townhouses: `realm/data/look.json`: `team.roofs.cottage`, `team.roofs.town` (shares, in that order), `team.roofs.thatch`, `team.roofs.dark_tile` (colours). The castle's and hall's roofs always wear the house's colour. How the thatch reads (straw bundles down the slope, each roof its own age, a ragged darker eave): `team.roofs.thatch_old` (the weathered colour) and `team.roofs.straw.*` (`fibre`, `clump`, `eave_dark`, `eave_band`, `storey`).
- The woods in autumn, per tree model: `realm/data/assets.json`: `woods.kit.seasons` (a tint over the leaves, MI_Leaves, per model file per season; a model not listed takes the season's own).
- The kit castle's gate: how high the portcullis hangs: `realm/data/assets.json`: `components.castle.kit[].spec.gate_portcullis` (metres; 2.4).
- How many houses a town raises, and the crown's seat packed tighter: `realm/data/towns.json`: `density.*`, `density.crown.*`.
- Towns along their roads in burgage plots instead of rings: `realm/data/towns.json`: `layout` (`plots`, the default; `rings`). The plots' depth of yard, gap between houses, lanes (how often, how long, how many by size, the most), how much a street wanders, how far the plots run, the ring of houses round the square, the yards' fence: `plots.*`; the crown's seat's own, and how many more houses it raises on plots: `plots.crown.*`. The yards' light fence model: `realm/data/assets.json`: `components.fence_yard`. How near something already standing (the square, the castle, a landmark, a house) a house must be to stand, so a town grows out from its heart with one clear edge and no house alone out on the fields: `plots.near` (0 turns it off). The crown's seat packed close round its castle and square (art target 01-town): `plots.crown.edge_more`, `lane_every`, `lane_length`, `yard`, and `wander` (lower: straighter streets, houses squarer to each other). A plot that would stand on another street or on a house already cut slides along its own street until it is clear (town_plots.gd), so corners hold a house instead of a gap. Each plotted town prints `CHECK town plots:` with its plots cut, houses stood and why the rest were turned away (another street, another house, the realm's road, crowded, water or a cliff, alone: never reached by the town); more lanes or `edge_more` when it falls short.
- The crown's market at midday (more stalls, buyers per stall, a smith, carters, porters, how many the square and each of its places hold, how many of its people are out, how many keep a city trade instead of farming): `realm/data/towns.json`: `market.crown.*`. Other towns keep the square's budget by size.
- What a lived-in square should measure, counted in 01-town (people on the setts, per 100 m² of open paving, the share standing, alone / in 2-4 / in 5+, the largest open stretch nobody stands near, the share by a stall, the well, the statue or a door, the largest knot of people standing about (the crowd is the census) and how much walking keeps to the same ways (desire paths)), and how near counts as near: `realm/data/towns.json`: `square.targets.*`, `square.near_m`. Errands that cross the open middle: the `errands` row's `open_stops` (how many open spots) and `open_apart` (m apart). - How deep a yard each house round the square keeps behind it (fences, a kitchen bed, a tree, barrels: the plots' own yard layer; 0 for none; shortened to half where a street runs behind): `realm/data/towns.json` `plots.ring_yard` (game units; the burgage plots' is `plots.yard`).
- Where the crown's square buildings stand on its edge, seen from the town camera: `realm/data/towns.json` `market.crown.lens_turns` (per landmark: a turn in radians round the square from the town camera's side (camera_rig.town_view's own lens) toward the frame's right, negative to its left; or {turn, face: "lens"} to turn its front to the camera, as the forge in the near right). - How far the barn's beasts keep off the square: `realm/data/works.json` `yard.off_square` (world units past its edge; their pen goes behind the barn, or out toward the fields, when it would fall on it).
- Where a ship in a port town lies: `realm/data/army.json` `map.moor_from` and `map.moor_reach` (from how far and to how far from the town's tile to look for water, beyond its houses, world units), `map.moor_off_lens` (radians round from the town view's lens side the water must lie; none there: the ship isn't drawn), `map.moor_off` (how far off the shore), `map.moor_gap` (between the ships of one town).

## The ground (NPC perf; art direction fix 5)
- The painted ground (the realm's one: the look audit's paintings, `realm/assets/ground/painted_v2`): `realm/data/ground.json`: each Terrain3D layer's painting and how often it repeats (`layers.<layer>.painting`, `.scale`, about 2 a game unit for the paintings' 4 m a tile; repack with `realm/tools/pack_painted_ground.gd`, then `--import`); each painting calmed before packing, WC3-style low contrast and muted colour (`layers.<layer>.calm`: `contrast`, `sat`, `mult`; docs/research/terrain-wc3-method.md); the grass's broad colour patches: `macro` (`a`, `b`, `scale`); near and far tiling: `dual.near`, `dual.far`; the earth showing where feet wear the ground: `wear.colour`.
- The ground by Freeciv's terrain (plains earthier, hills stony, desert sand, swamp mud, tundra bare earth, forest floor): `realm/data/ground.json` `terrain.<terrain>` (each layer's weight: grass, dirt, sand, snow, rock, forest, mud); the worn earth through the grass: `terrain.patches` (`most`: how bare at a patch's heart; `from`: higher, fewer patches; `soft`: how far they fray; `scale`: higher, smaller patches).
- The coast: how far the beach runs above the waterline and which painting it blends through (dirt, no outline), and the surf at the shore: `realm/data/ground.json`: `beach.top`, `surf`.
